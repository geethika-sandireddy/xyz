Genuinely, mixed reaction — real strengths, but a real risk given your track record. Let's go through it honestly.

**What's actually good about the idea:**
- It's not another "chat with your PDF" clone — there's a decision-making layer (flag, estimate, recommend action), which is a step above the typical AI-wrapper projects that flood every recruiter's inbox.
- Real utility, easy to explain in one sentence, demo-able with your own bank statement — that's genuinely valuable, since "can you show me it working" is exactly what kills unfinished portfolio projects.

**The real risk, and I need to be blunt about this because of your history:**

Look at your own repo pattern so far: `financial-doc-tool` had a `.env` scare and 3 commits, `banking-dashboard` had hardcoded credentials and committed `.class` files, `social-media-intrusion-detection-system` sat at 2 commits with "under development" left in the README for months. Your pattern isn't "bad ideas" — it's **scope exceeding follow-through.**

This project as scoped is genuinely large: two distinct AI-powered flows, a crowd-sourced database, 7 API endpoints, 5 pages, PDF parsing, a full Postgres schema with Drizzle ORM. For someone building solo without a team, this is realistically 3-4 weeks of focused work to do *well*, not just to get running. If your history repeats — and I say this as the person who's watched it happen three times this conversation already — you'll end up with another repo that's 60% scaffolded, thin on polish, and technically "started" rather than finished. A half-built version of this is worse for your credibility than your current three clean, finished small projects.

**One more honest flag:** the "crowd-learning" and "community price data" feature is a real weakness as a *portfolio* project specifically — it only has value with real users submitting real prices. As a solo project demo, that data will be empty or fabricated, and a sharp interviewer asking "how many real price submissions does this have" will expose that it's a feature that doesn't actually work yet, which can read worse than not having the feature at all.
